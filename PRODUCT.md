# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Enterprise knowledge-base users who need to upload, search, and question internal documents during focused work. They expect the assistant to expose sources, preserve context across sessions, and make advanced agent modes understandable without turning the workspace into a developer-only tool.

## Product Purpose

Enterprise AI Agent helps users retrieve trusted answers from internal knowledge, inspect citations, invoke approved tools, and use RAG, ReAct, and plan-and-execute workflows in a production-oriented web workspace. Success means users can move from document ingestion to grounded answers with confidence, clear feedback, and minimal ceremony.

## Brand Personality

Professional, trustworthy, clear, and operational. The interface should feel capable and composed, with enough visual energy to avoid flatness while staying serious enough for enterprise work.

## Anti-references

Avoid toy-like AI chat styling, generic SaaS-template gloss, gray and lifeless utility screens, terminal-heavy hacker aesthetics, decorative complexity, and consumer-chat patterns that hide source traceability or operational state.

## Design Principles

- Make evidence visible: citations, trace IDs, tool results, and agent steps should be easy to find and scan.
- Keep the workspace task-first: prioritize document work, session context, and answer composition over decorative framing.
- Show operational confidence: status, permissions, roles, metrics, and errors should feel deliberate and trustworthy.
- Preserve density without fatigue: enterprise users need compact data, but hierarchy and contrast must prevent the UI from becoming gray noise.
- Explain advanced agent behavior through structure: ReAct steps, plan execution, and tool calls should read as inspectable workflow artifacts, not mysterious side output.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and interactive states. Provide visible focus states, readable status colors that do not rely on color alone, reduced-motion fallbacks for animation, and layouts that remain usable on smaller screens without hiding critical document, chat, or citation workflows.
